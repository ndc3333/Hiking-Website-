About Me

Hi! My name is Nihal and I love hiking. I started walking in the mountains two years ago and now I go almost every weekend. I made this page to practice HTML and CSS and to share what I have learned.

My Trails

Lake Loop

A short and easy walk around a quiet lake. It takes about two hours, so it is great for beginners. Bring a snack for the picnic spot!

Pine Forest Path

This trail goes through tall pine trees and it smells amazing. It is a bit longer, but the ground is soft and shady.

Hiking Tips

Here are a few things I always remember before I go out:

  • Wear comfortable shoes
  • Bring water and a snack
  • Check the weather first
  • Tell someone where you are going
